# Not claimed

This draft does not do any of the following.

- It does not diagnose anyone.
- It does not interpret drawings, words, or silence.
- It does not claim to know what a person feels.
- It does not offer treatment, therapy, or clinical care.

Weekly noticing, when that screen exists, stays a tag count plus this question: "What do you make of that?"

The draft does not answer that question for the person.
